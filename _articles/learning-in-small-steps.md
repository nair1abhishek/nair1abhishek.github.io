A new subject can feel enormous. One way to make it manageable is to begin with a small question and follow it until you can explain the answer in your own words.

## Start with something concrete

Choose a problem small enough to explore in an afternoon. Make a prediction, try it, and write down what surprised you.

## Keep the useful bits

A short note can capture a command, a mental model, or a link worth revisiting. Later, several notes may become the beginnings of an article.
